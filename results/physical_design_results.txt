Notes
-----

The ALU4 design is combinational and does not contain
registers or flip-flops. Therefore, a meaningful clock-tree
synthesis (CTS) stage is not applicable.

Standalone OpenROAD timing analysis was not used for the
reported results because the OpenROAD session did not have
Liberty timing libraries loaded.

The reported design area should not be interpreted as the
complete die area.
